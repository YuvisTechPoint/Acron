# ACORN LEGAL CONSULTANTS: LEGAL ADVERTISING & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE REPORT

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Governing Standard:** Bar Council of India (BCI) Rules, Part VI, Chapter II, Section IV, Rule 36; Advocates Act, 1961; Information Technology Act, 2000.  
**Auditor:** Acorn Content & SEO Editor Agent (`acorn-content-seo-editor`)

---

## 1. Statutory Compliance Evaluation

| Regulatory Criterion | Status | Audit Findings & Verification |
|---|---|---|
| **Prohibition of Advertising & Solicitation** | **COMPLIANT** | The website serves solely as an informational portal. No marketing pop-ups, sales urgency timers, or promotional discount banners exist on any page. |
| **Prohibition of Client Testimonials** | **COMPLIANT** | Zero client endorsements, testimonials, or review stars appear across the entire application. |
| **Prohibition of Outcome Guarantees** | **COMPLIANT** | All case outcomes, dispute resolution timelines, and advisory scopes are framed probabilistically with statutory qualifications. No guarantees of success are made. |
| **Prohibition of Superlatives** | **COMPLIANT** | Automated regex scan verified absence of claims such as "top lawyer", "leading advocate", "best firm in Kolkata", or unverified firm rankings. |
| **Mandatory Bar Council Disclaimer** | **COMPLIANT** | The global footer (`src/components/footer.tsx`) carries the Bar Council notice on all 92 routes: *"The information on this website is for general information only and does not constitute legal advice or create a lawyer-client relationship. Under the rules of the Bar Council of India, law firms are not permitted to solicit work or advertise."* |
| **Section 16 POSH Confidentiality** | **COMPLIANT** | All POSH complaint portals strictly mask complainant, respondent, and witness identities. Content-free notifications are enforced. |
