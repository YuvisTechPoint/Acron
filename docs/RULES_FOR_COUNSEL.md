## 2. Internal Committee (IC) Quorum & Composition Rules

Under Section 4(2) of the POSH Act:
1. **Presiding Officer:** Must be a woman employed at a senior level at the workplace from amongst employees.
2. **Employee Members:** Not less than two members from amongst employees preferably committed to the cause of women or having legal experience/social work knowledge.
3. **External Member:** At least one member from an NGO or association committed to the cause of women, or a person familiar with sexual harassment issues.
4. **Gender Quota:** At least **one-half (50%)** of total IC members must be women.
5. **Hearing Quorum:** Minimum of three members, including the Presiding Officer and the External Member, required to convene any valid evidentiary hearing.

## 3. Statutory Penalties & Disclosures

- **Section 16 Non-Disclosure:** Strict bar on publishing or communicating the identity of complainant, respondent, witnesses, or inquiry details.
- **Section 17 Breach Penalty:** Penalty in accordance with service rules or ₹5,000 fine for disclosure.
- **Section 26 Employer Penalties:** Fine of up to ₹50,000 for failure to constitute IC or act on recommendations; repeat offences attract doubled penalty and potential cancellation/revocation of corporate registration/business licenses.
