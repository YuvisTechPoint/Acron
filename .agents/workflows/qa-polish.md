# /qa-polish (run last)

Act as a ruthless senior design reviewer. Use the browser agent.
1. Open every route at 360, 768, 1280 and 1536 px. Take screenshots and attach them to the walkthrough.
2. Record a scroll-through of the homepage, one service page, one article and the request form.
3. Find and fix: misaligned elements, inconsistent spacing, orphaned words, text under 16px on mobile, low contrast, missing focus states, layout shift, any non-token colours, banned motion, and any section that feels like a template.
4. Run Lighthouse and axe. Targets: performance 90+, accessibility 95+, LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1. Fix and re-run until met.
5. Test reduced motion and keyboard-only navigation of the header, mega-menu, forms and accordion.
6. Confirm no testimonials, results claims, rankings or invented statistics, and that the disclaimer is on every page.
7. Write a before-and-after summary and a list of any remaining design debt.
