5. WHY ACORN: three columns on stone background, each a principle (Integrity, Strategic insight, Commercial understanding) with an italic serif sentence. No statistics, no logos, no testimonials.
6. POSH FEATURE: a dark navy panel with an arch image placeholder, heading "Safe, compliant workplaces", short text, buttons "Explore POSH Compliance" and "Report a concern". Calm tone.
7. INSIGHTS: one large featured article card plus two smaller ones.
8. FAQ PREVIEW: four questions in an accordion on the left, a "Still have a question?" card on the right.
9. CLOSING CTA: ink-950 band with an arch, headline "Let us understand your matter.", primary brass button and a phone link.
