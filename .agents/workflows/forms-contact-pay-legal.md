# /forms-contact-pay-legal

Build /request-service, /contact, /pay, the legal pages and /404.
REQUEST SERVICE: split layout. Left: a 3-step form (About you, Your matter, Review and send) with a brass progress stepper, 52px inputs, inline validation, file upload with progress, consent checkbox, and an animated success state that shows the reference ID. Right: a sticky reassurance panel on navy with "We respond within one business day", confidentiality note and call link.
CONTACT: three information cards, a stylised map placeholder card with "Open in Google Maps", office hours, a short form.
PAY: a focused, minimal checkout in three steps with a trust note about Razorpay, clear amount display in INR, and status pages for success, failed and pending with calm wording.
LEGAL PAGES: clean typography, sticky table of contents, effective date and version labels.
404 and 500: an elegant arch illustration, helpful links, search and a contact button.
All forms: keyboard accessible, aria-live results, no CAPTCHA friction (honeypot plus invisible check).
